# A2b preregistration — Boolean timeline maximization and diversity baselines

**Registered 2026-10-09 before generating any candidate timelines.** Newly introduced numeric criteria in this protocol are **written after A0-R2 and A1 results**, and are justified here by the exact-uniform sampler/support calculation rather than measurements from these candidate seeds. No frame pools, assembled traces, ns-3, or protocol variants in A2b.

## Cells, generator, seeds, and timeout

Use F=120 and lives `{0.3,0.5,0.7,0.9}`. Select stability by A2a's frozen default; its result is recorded in `alpha/gates.md`: respectively `.3,.5,.7,.9`, all `s_div` and feasible. Timeline behavior is the hashed A0-R2 isolated `behavior="patched"` implementation. Timeline Boolean schedules depend only on F, life, stability, and seed, not graph family; generate each life/seed once and join the identical serialized schedule to all five A1 N=92 family rows. Test equality byte-for-byte across the five family labels for each seed/life; any mismatch is a failure.

For each of the 4 life cells, generate all 200 seeds 42–241; each cell-level candidate batch is a child process with a 60-second wall timeout. Timeout, exception, or a missing candidate is failure with no retry. Record per-life `config.json` with all args, candidate seeds, A1 graph IDs/edge hashes, A0R2 tree and module hashes, Python version, and generator label `yadygaga_patched`.

## Level 1 candidate statistics and selection

For schedules x,y of length F, define H as the differing-bit fraction and normalized D as `H/(2*min(q,1-q))`, q=U/F. All A2c lives are nontrivial. Selection never inspects graph frames or ns-3 data.

For each life:

- **Max-min greedy selection:** from the candidate pool, first select seed 42; repeatedly choose the candidate maximizing its minimum D to all selected candidates, ties by lowest seed. Select a prefix of 10, report the 5-prefix and 10-set, selected seed order, minimum pairwise D, and mean pairwise D.
- **Comparison:** scan ascending candidate seed and retain the first five pairwise-distinct schedules; record their seed IDs and the same min/mean D. Record every candidate schedule hash and status/reason, including duplicate references and seeds after the selected set size where applicable.
- Calculate pair statistics for the 5-set and 10-set: number unique, mean/min pairwise D, raw Hamming mean, and fraction of unordered pairs with D<0.10. For all 200 candidates report unique count and largest identical-schedule group.
- Report `mean D / D_independent`, where `D_independent=max(q,1-q)`. Also report exact-uniform `E[D]`, MC standard error, `mean D / E[D_exact-uniform]`, and selected-pair statistics relative to that baseline.

### Exact-uniform expectation method

Estimate each cell's exact-uniform expected pairwise D with **100,000 independent schedule pairs**, each schedule generated by the exact-uniform patched sampler at the A2a-selected `(F,life,stability)`. Monte Carlo seed pair i uses `1,000,000+2i` and `1,000,001+2i`, i=0..99,999. Report sample mean and standard error (`sample SD / sqrt(100000)`). Since normalized D is bounded by 1 for fixed nontrivial uptime, this sample size implies worst-case standard error <=0.001582; preregistered validity tolerance is **SE <=0.002**. Do not use the candidate seed pool to estimate this baseline.

## Numeric diversity rule (maximization plus baselines)

A cell is labelled “diverse” only if all apply:

1. At least 5 unique schedules exist in the 200 candidates and the chosen 5-set is pairwise distinct.
2. Max-min greedy selected 5-set mean D is at least **0.90 × exact-uniform expected mean D**. The 0.90 multiplier is **written after A0-R2/A1 results** as a 10% finite-candidate tolerance from the exact-uniform attainable baseline; it is not an absolute D cutoff.
3. Largest duplicate schedule group among 200 candidates is at most **20** (10% of 200). This post-A0-R2/A1 threshold is a detectable gross concentration guardrail; the first-5-distinct comparison does not substitute for it.
4. Level 2 canonical route-sequence distinctness at A2c: among the 10 unordered pairs in the selected 5-set, at least `ceil(0.90 * (1 - 1/M) * 10)` pairs must have different full 120-frame canonical route sequences. Here M is exact support from A2a for the cell. Under independent exact-uniform schedule draws, `1-1/M` is the exact probability the Boolean schedules are distinct; 0.90 permits at most a 10% shortfall from that baseline in route-sequence distinction. This is evaluated with the canonical sorted-label shortest-path rule in A2c. Report NetworkX insertion-order route differences separately; they do not replace the canonical gate.

A failing cell is reported as “diversity-limited by construction” with M; criteria are never relaxed. Family ranking from Level 2 values is descriptive only. Means/SD are reported over seeds with sample SD ddof=1 where applicable. This is a maximization/relative-baseline criterion; do not apply the previous absolute mean D>=.30 rule.

## Gate and scope

A2b passes its operational gate if all 800 candidates are generated without timeout, same-seed schedules match exactly across family labels, greedy and first-five-distinct selections and all summary statistics are output, the 100,000-pair MC standard error meets .002, all 4 config files and candidate seed/hash records are present, and results/hashes are appended to `alpha/gates.md` before A2c preregistration. The per-cell diversity verdict also incorporates A2c's Level 2 route criterion and must be reported in A2c; no claim is made before it is measured. No A2c work begins until the A2b operational gate is recorded.
