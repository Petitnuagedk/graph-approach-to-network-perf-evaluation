## Model and exact parameters

For each undirected skeleton edge, sample a two-state binary Markov chain independently. Let `P(1→0)=1/L` and `P(0→1)=π/((1−π)L)`; initialize in stationary Bernoulli(π). These transitions imply stationary edge-up fraction π and mean geometric up-spell duration L. Register the 2×2 diagnostic grid `π∈{0.3,0.7}`, `L∈{3,8}` frames; all four parameter combinations are feasible because `π/((1−π)L)≤1`. Run three independent seeds `{11,29,47}` for 120,000 frames per combination on a one-edge graph. Measure empirical edge-up fraction over all frames and mean *completed* up-run length, excluding left/right censored spells.

Standalone-chain numeric acceptance, fixed before the test: for every one of 12 seed×parameter traces, absolute empirical π error <=.02 and relative mean-up-duration error <=.10. Report per-seed values, pooled mean and sample SD (`ddof=1`) across the three seeds per grid cell, transition probabilities, and full generated chain hashes. These tolerances are sampling-precision guards for 120,000 frames, not target-fit tuning.
