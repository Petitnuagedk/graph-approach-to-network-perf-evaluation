# A2g-3 preregistration — integrate edge memory with `uk` timelines

Registered after A2g-2's H2 NOT SUPPORTED outcome was logged and before any A2g-3 runner or trace operation. Offline/generator-only only. New files and outputs remain under `alpha/A2g/`; no changes to A0/A0R/A0R2/A1/A2/A2f donor sources/results or `alpha/EM/`. No ns-3, protocol variants, or A3.

## Question and frozen grid

Measure whether the A2d item-1 per-edge stationary Markov sampler plus deterministic S–R projection can preserve its registered edge-statistic tolerances when driven by the adopted A2f `uk` exact-quota timeline interface at two lives, while varying memory and edge marginal. Use five A1 realization-1 N=92 graphs and their frozen structural-rule probe pairs; F=120; lives `{.5,.9}` with exact `U={60,108}`; fixed mean-up duration 12 frames with `K={5,9}` respectively; `pathPersistency=.75`; three operation seeds `{11,29,47}` per design cell. Edge parameters: `(π,L)={(.5,2),(.5,8),(.8,8)}`. The omitted `(π=.8,L=2)` cell is mathematically infeasible because `p(0→1)=π/((1−π)L)=2>1`; record it as infeasible and do not launch or substitute it. Feasible grid size is 5 families × 2 lives × 3 feasible `(π,L)` combinations × 3 seeds = **90 isolated operations**, corresponding to 30 family×life×parameter cells with three seeds each.

Use graph/probe pairs: geodesic `(0,31)`, RGG `(14,47)`, WS `(49,85)`, ER `(5,82)`, BA `(5,72)`. Treat the A1 skeleton as an undirected graph with all 92 nodes, including isolates. Generate each timeline through the read-only A2f `behavior="uk"` overlay with exact integer U/K; pass those bits directly to the A2d item-1 `behavior="patched-v3"` sampler. Do not generate a separate timeline per graph/parameter combination from a different source or relax exact quotas. Timeline seed is the operation seed. Sampler seed is `seed+10000`, matching the A2f-3 frame-seed offset. Record the actual schedule bits/hash and every source/graph/config hash.

The operation uses the sampler's returned constrained sequence directly; it does not draw from finite up/down pools or use the replacement assembler. Save both raw Markov frames and projected frames, changed-edge list per frame, timeline bits/path IDs, per-edge/per-frame states, and per-trace metrics. Record instantaneous S–R mask, path and skeleton provenance, raw and projected edge-up fractions, raw and projected completed up-duration estimates, and edge-time projection fraction. No waiting or packet oracle is evaluated. Any time-respecting reachability, if included as a descriptive side metric, must be labelled “allows store-carry-forward”.

## Carried numeric thresholds and H3 gate

Keep A2d item-1 numeric tolerances unchanged for every operation: exact requested S–R mask and exactly U connected frames; every sampled edge lies in the skeleton; projection-changed edge-time fraction `<=.05`; projected pooled edge-up fraction absolute error from π `<=.05`; projected pooled completed up-duration relative error from L `<=.20`. Also report raw unprojected π and duration values separately. The A2d item-1 standalone-chain checks (`|π_hat−π|<=.02` and completed up-duration relative error `<=.10`) are reported as descriptive reference only here because each edge has only a 120-frame segment in the integrated trace; they are not repurposed into a new per-edge gate.

A design cell qualifies only when all three registered seeds complete and **each of the three** passes all five carried per-operation criteria above. H3 passes iff all 90 operations complete within their limits and at least **27 of 30 cells (90%)** qualify. This 90% cell rule is frozen before A2g-3 measurements: A2d item 1 had 60/60 integrated graph×parameter×seed traces pass its unchanged tolerances; the transfer gate therefore demands near-universal cell-level replication while allowing at most 3 of 30 cells to miss under the new life/parameter grid. It does not permit a failed seed to be averaged away. Also report exact pass counts for each criterion and each life/parameter stratum. No threshold may be changed after generation.
