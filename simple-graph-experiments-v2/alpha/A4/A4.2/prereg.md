## Frozen readiness, measurement and outcomes

Readiness is computed independently from each run’s `packets_<protocol>.csv`, matching probe Tx/Rx by the probe app’s scheduled `app_tx_time_s` (first probe Tx at warm-up+0.1 s; 1,024-byte packets at 50 kbps, 0.16384-s interval). Apply the A4.1 detector unchanged: unique matched probe receptions; G=1 s; trailing 10-s window; minimum observation age 10 s from first probe Tx; first event in the final contiguous passing suffix. Readiness is absolute simulation-clock seconds; undefined remains undefined.

A run is `readiness_after_warmup` iff readiness is undefined or strictly greater than 120 s. This flag is descriptive and does not gate execution. Full measurement interval uses the A3 flow accounting over the 120-frame measurement period. The post-readiness interval is from readiness through the simulation stop and is computed only if readiness is finite and strictly before stop. Post-readiness PDR counts only scheduled packets whose app Tx is in that interval and unique matched Rx for those packet identities by stop. For background flows report type-7 median and p10 across per-flow PDRs; probe PDR is its unique delivery fraction. If timing records cannot identify a flow/packet unambiguously, mark that subwindow metric not derivable, do not impute. Report full-window and subwindow metrics separately.

Cell PDR pass is probe PDR ≥0.99 and background per-flow median PDR ≥0.95 in the full measurement window. Borderline is descriptive only: absolute distance from either threshold ≤0.02. A flagged-run PDR shortfall is classified `convergence-affected`, not routing/MAC, unless a full-window shortfall also occurs in an unflagged run at the same protocol × N × phi; that exception is reported as shared-load shortfall and its measured evidence shown. For unflagged runs, use only the preregistered MAC attribution thresholds: max-degree-node retry callback ratio >0.20 and ≥3× the same node’s phi=1% ratio, or max per-node collision/interference PHY Rx drop rate ≥1/s and ≥3× its phi=1% rate. Zero-baseline comparisons require a positive high-load measure and absolute thresholds remain binding. Report no causal claim beyond that fixed classification. Queue/no-route/route-error counts are descriptive supporting counters.

Load grid is exactly `{1%, 5%, 10%}` for each retained protocol/family/size; no 2% runs in A4.2. The additional 2% load is conditional on a separately justified future resolution of a 5% failure. All thresholds are fixed before execution; all extra diagnostics are labeled **“written after A4.2 results”**.
