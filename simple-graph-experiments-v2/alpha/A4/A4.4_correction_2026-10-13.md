# A4.4 correction — 2026-10-13

## Finding

A4.4 was not a static all-links-up run. The runs replayed time-varying frames supplied through `frames_csv`. The `timeline_audit.csv` evidence records 30 of 30 runs with time-varying inputs and 15 unique frame sets; the first and second blocks differ in those frame sets. Thus the A4.4 execution does not implement the registered static definition “all links up for the whole run.”

The A4.4 producer and the dynamic parameters used to create those frames were not recorded. Consequently, the provenance needed to reconstruct or interpret the dynamic replay is incomplete. B0 gates G2 and G3 failed for this reason: the build/configuration identity and the dynamic-input provenance are not established.

## Disposition

A4.4 is retained as a historical record and relabelled **dynamic frame replay with unrecorded producer and parameters**. It is not a static baseline and is not the Beta reference. No A4.4 artifact, preregistration, report, budget entry, or run result is changed by this correction.

The earlier statement in the A4.4 report that called A4.4 a “static baseline” is superseded by this correction. The A4.4 results remain historical observations of the replayed runs, not evidence for the static reference or for Beta.

## Replacement reference

A4.5 is introduced as the pending true static all-links-up baseline for AODV and OLSR at N=42, 92, and 162. Its exact definition, seeds, and frame construction will be fixed in the A4.5 preregistration. This correction performs no simulation and does not authorize A4.5, B0, B1, or any other run.
