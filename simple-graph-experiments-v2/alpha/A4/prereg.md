## Frozen numeric decision rules

1. **DSDV all-up outage:** gap is the longest interval between consecutive uniquely delivered probe packets after the first delivery. If any gap is strictly greater than 5.0 s, mark that DSDV graph cell `all_up_outage`, record no-route, queue, PHY and MAC counters, and exclude that DSDV graph/protocol cell from later load/replication metrics. A gap exactly 5.0 s does not trigger. Explicitly compare with the Phase 6D 15 s outage.
2. **Convergence censoring:** the A3 detector uses trailing 10 s windows with unique received probe packets / offered probe packets ≥.99, evaluated at Tx/Rx event times and 10 s expiries, with every later full window through simulation end also passing. A convergence value >180 s or NaN is censored; no warm-up is derived for that protocol,N if any of its five family-r1 runs is censored.
3. **MAC contention attribution:** at the higher load, report a PDR drop as MAC-contention-associated (not a routing effect) iff either (i) the max-degree node's `MacTxDataFailed` retry callbacks divided by its app-data-frame count exceed .20 and are at least 3× the same node's lowest-load ratio, or (ii) its maximum per-node collision/interference PHY Rx drops per simulated second are at least 3× the lowest-load rate and at least 1.0/s. App-data-frame denominator is source-flow offered packets at that node plus its A3b-counted forwarded app packets; link retries are not counted as extra app frames. The numerator is the A3 `MacTxDataFailed` callback count; terminal/final MAC and queue drops are reported separately. Collision rate denominator is the complete simulated interval including warmup, because the registered instrumentation does not timestamp PHY-drop events. If lowest-load denominator is zero, the threefold clause is satisfied only by a positive higher-load value; the absolute floors remain binding.
4. **10% saturation:** trigger if median background per-flow PDR <.50, max-node retry ratio >.30, or the preliminary projected 4.2 wall time >24 h. Any trigger selects per-node load for 4.2; otherwise use per-pair phi. The three fallback κ roles and exact mapping are frozen above. A fallback is not permission to exceed the variant cap.
5. **Borderline:** descriptive only when `abs(probe_PDR-.99) ≤ .02` or `abs(background_median_PDR-.95) ≤ .02`; it never changes pass/fail.
6. **PDR gates:** a measured load cell passes iff probe PDR ≥.99 and median background per-flow PDR ≥.95 after warmup. At a given graph/protocol, failure at the lowest load stops its higher-load cells and records the measured cause (no route, MAC/contention, queue, all-up outage, or other). A protocol is formally dropped at N=162 iff all five family-r1 cells fail at the lowest load. No PDR rescaling or static reference.
7. **Per-cell summaries:** PDR is unique delivered / offered per flow. Quantiles use linear type-7 interpolation. Delay gate for validation requires median ≤ p95 and at least one delivered packet. Gini uses the A1 population formula, with 0 for all-zero vectors. Missing/NaN values are retained, never imputed. Single-run cells have no SD. Replication SD is sample SD (`ddof=1`) across the available r1/r2/r3 graph realizations only; replay seeds are not realizations.
8. **Operating load:** per size choose the highest of the tested roles {1,5,10}% for which every non-dropped protocol × family-r1 cell that passed at the lowest role passes at that role. If 1% passes for at least one retained cell but 5% is not an eligible common load, run the additional 2% grid at that size only (5 families × 3 protocols; skipped already-dropped cells are listed). The 2% variants are charged to 4.3 first; replication variants are cut in this order: r3 (second extra realization) across random families, then r2, resolving ties by family order RGG, WS, ER, BA and protocol order OLSR, AODV, DSDV. Sizes without a 2% grid are not cut until those replications are exhausted. If no common load exists, record “none”; do not select by judgement.
