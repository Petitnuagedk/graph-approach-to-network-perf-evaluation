# Alpha study limitations

Carry these limitations into later Alpha stage reports by citing this file rather than restating the list.

- A2f exact-quota life and mean up-run are confounded under the A2a stability rule. `uk` decouples the request interface but does not expand the finite exact-quota support or establish protocol behavior.
- Timeline diversity is family-independent by construction; the A2b gate is non-binding by greedy max-min selection, and Level 2 route diversity is tie-break-sensitive.
- The memory sampler is a patched, non-upstream copy. A2g-2's legacy iid finite-pool source is distinct from A2g-3's adopted `uk` + patched-v3 sequence.
- A2g graph coverage uses one A1 realization per family/size and fixed structural-rule probe pairs. Stored A2f-3 versus A2g-3 traces are not matched-seed source comparisons.
- A2g-4 availability contrasts are descriptive conditional associations. They do not establish a causal sampler mechanism; repeated edges/frames are not independent observations.
- A2g-2 supply thresholds count distinct finite-pool entries and are stricter than the legacy replacement assembler's nonempty-pool requirement. Observed p* boundaries are sample/grid-specific; undefined or beyond-boundary supply is not evidence of physical impossibility.
- Time-respecting reachability allows store-carry-forward and is not a routing protocol oracle. Alpha's adopted oracle is instantaneous per-frame usable S–R uptime with no waiting.
- No Alpha generator-only stage establishes routing-protocol performance. Avoid causal claims, family rankings, or ceiling language unless a later preregistered design validly supports them.
